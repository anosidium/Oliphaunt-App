# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Oliphaunt handles information.

Oliphaunt is developed and maintained by an independent developer. The app is designed with privacy in mind and does not collect personal data beyond what is strictly necessary for it to function correctly and to support ongoing development.

Oliphaunt does not track you across other apps or websites, does not profile you and does not share any information for marketing or advertising purposes.

---

### Information You Provide

#### Mastodon Accounts

You are not required to sign in to use Oliphaunt. The app can browse remote Mastodon instances without an account.

If you choose to sign in, authentication takes place directly between your device and the Mastodon instance you select. Your account credentials are never transmitted to the developer.

Access tokens issued by your Mastodon instance are stored securely in the macOS Keychain. These tokens are used solely to perform authorised requests on your behalf.

#### Local Data

Cached posts, preferences and other app settings are stored locally on your device.

This data is not transmitted to the developer.

---

### Analytics

Oliphaunt uses [TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com) to collect anonymised, privacy-preserving analytics. TelemetryDeck gathers high-level signals that help assess app's performance and general usage patterns, such as:

- feature usage
- app version
- anonymised device environment information such as system appearance, language and macOS version

TelemetryDeck does not collect personally identifiable information, does not track you across other apps or services and does not create user profiles.

Further details are available in the TelemetryDeck's [privacy policy](https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/).

---

### Crash Reporting

Oliphaunt uses [Sentry](https://sentry.io/) to collect crash reports. These reports include technical information about the state of the app at the time of a crash (such as stack traces and OS version) in order to diagnose and resolve faults.

Further details are available in the Sentry’s [privacy policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/).

---

### Network Communication

All communication with Mastodon instances takes place directly between your device and the instances you have chosen, using secure HTTPS connections.

No information is routed through servers owned or controlled by the developer.

The latest version will always be available in the repository from which this document is accessed.
